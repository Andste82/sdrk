# Open items

Items that remain open, are not covered by a later milestone, and need a decision or input that cannot be taken autonomously. Format: milestone, item, why it is open, what decision or input is needed. The items of M23 and M24 were added on 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01. Reviewed on 2026-10-24, after the SIMD optimization (pull request 17) and its CI run: the items that only asked for a first green CI run (M04, M08 to M12, M14-2) are closed because the jobs ran and passed on all three hosts.

M13-4 (the release 0.1) is closed by M25.1 on 2026-11-02 (D202): 0.1.0 is released with the waivers of `benches/limits.json` as they stand. Waivers that stay open with a reference: issue #71 (`fir_cq15_fallback` at `simd128`, `fir_decimate_cq15_fallback` at `sse2`, `sse4.2`, `avx2` and `neon`, `fir_interpolate_real_fallback` at `simd128`), I24-1 (`stereo_pll_fallback` at `sse2`, `sse4.2`, `avx2`, `neon` and `simd128`) and I24-3 (`stereo_matrix_fallback` at `sse2`). Accepted waivers, whose text states why the gap is accepted: `agc_fallback` at `sse2`, `sse4.2` and `neon` (the reference does less work), the `accuracy_waiver` of `fir_decimate_fallback` and `deemphasis_fallback` at every level (the reference computes in another accuracy class), `convert_cq15_cu8_fallback` on `neon`, and the `avx2-runtime` entries of `fir_cq15`, `fir_interpolate_cq15` and `fir_interpolate_real_q15` (the run-time build keeps the SIMD branch). `cargo xtask release-check` lists them.

I39.1-1 and I39.1-2 (test 5 of M39.1, ADS-B frames above 10 dB, and test 4, the OOK loop) are closed on 2026-11-06 by the maintainer's decisions D726 to D728: the `ddc` of `ook_receiver` has the cutoff 15 kHz (the 10 kHz offset of test 4 stays in the pass band), with a transition of 10 kHz added by the implementer (D726), and `ook_demod` puts its threshold at 0.45 of the swing between the tracked levels (D727); test 4 runs on the shipped flow at the values of M39.1 and measures PER 0 (500 of 500; 0.010 before). Neither test is ignored any more.

I43-1, I43-2, I43-3 and I43-4 (the BER of QPSK through `Multipath::severe` with a linear equalizer, the `mse` convergence of test 4, the position of the equalizer before or after the carrier loop, and the library gate of `equalize`) are closed on 2026-11-06 by the maintainer's decision to adapt the solutions of other projects, implemented as D825 to D838: `psk_demod` runs the equalizer between `symbol_sync` and `costas`, the order of GNU Radio's PSK tutorial and liquid-dsp's blind equalizer example (D825); both updates are normalized by the power of the filter window as liquid-dsp's `eqlms` and GNU Radio's `adaptive_algorithm_nlms` do (D826), the CMA error is clipped as in GNU Radio's `adaptive_algorithm_cma` (D827), the defaults are `mu_cma` 0.05, `mu_dd` 0.02 and 1000 CMA symbols (D828); a decision-directed phase tracker inside the kernel gives the decision mode its phase before `costas` (D829, an own decision); a decision feedback part (`feedback_taps`, `equalizer_feedback_taps`) cancels the postcursors that a linear equalizer cannot undo at the spectral null of `severe` (D830); `equalize_fallback` is gated against a C translation of GNU Radio's `linear_equalizer` with `adaptive_algorithm_nlms` (D838). Measured with the thresholds of the specification unchanged: test 2 with 15 forward and 5 feedback taps no error in 10^6 bits and no slip (edge 9.0e-9; the linear equalizer 5.5e-4; without the equalizer 9.3e-2), test 3 no error in 10^6 bits (edge 8.7e-7), test 4 `mse` below -20 dB after symbol 1029 (limit 2000) with a CMA phase spread of 0.0104 rad (D834 to D836).

I40-1 (the RRC matched-pair bound of test 1 of M40) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D626: `design::rrc` stays the truncated root-raised cosine of the specification, the bound of the test is 1e-2 for span 10 and more (the 1e-3 of the specification was an error: the RRC designs of GNU Radio and MATLAB leave the same 1.5e-3 to 6.1e-3), and optimized square-root Nyquist designs are in the backlog.

I40.1-1, I40.1-2 and I40.1-3 (the order-8 lock metric of `costas`, the pi/4-DQPSK envelope bound and the OQPSK receiver of M40.1) are closed on 2026-11-06 by the maintainer's decisions D659 and D685 to D688: the order-8 lock metric is `cos(8 err) = T4(cos(2 err))` with a fixed operation order, so order 8 locks on 8PSK and stays unlocked on noise (D659, the golden case `costas/8psk_lock_c32_8192` regenerated); the pi/4-DQPSK envelope bound of test 3 is `min |y| >= 0.12` for roll-off 0.35 over all samples (measured 0.135, D685); `psk_demod` with `offset` derotates the matched-filter output sample by sample with its own carrier loop before the half-symbol realignment and uses the OQPSK phase detector of the in-phase and quadrature parts half a symbol apart (D686, D687), and test 4 runs with the carrier offset and drift of M40 (OQPSK 1.03 and 1.11 times plain QPSK at 6 and 8 dB) plus a test with constant carrier phases and the acquisition (D688).

I37-1 (the dependency `sdrk-io` → `sdrk-proto` against the R2 row of `crates/io`) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D528: `sdrk-io` has its own record type `PacketRecord` and does not depend on `sdrk-proto`, and `cargo xtask check layers` allows `crates/io` the crates of `crates/core` only.

I34-1 (a de-emphasis for the stereo receiver built from primitives) is closed on 2026-11-04 by the maintainer's decisions D396 to D398: the kind `deemphasis` takes complex stereo audio too, the flow `fm_stereo_receiver_primitives` ends with it and equals `fm_stereo_demod` bit for bit; an explicit-coefficient mode for `biquad` is in the backlog.

I33-1 and I33-2 (the `noise_blank` kernel rules and block defaults) are closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decisions D425 and D426: the block defaults are a lookahead of 10 us and no hold, the values of the flow `hf_am_receiver`, and the kernel rules D363 to D365 stand.

I36-1 (the limiter of the FM stereo transmitter at 48 kHz before the resampler overshot the deviation) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D487: the flow `fm_stereo_transmitter` runs the limiter after the resampler, at 192 kHz, as `fm_transmitter` does; a full-scale 15 kHz burst stays at a multiplex peak of 0.990 and 74.2 kHz deviation.

I38-1 (the loop gain of `symbol_sync` depended on `sps`) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D565 (option b): the kernel reads the detector output as a timing error in symbols and scales `kp` and `ki` by `sps`, so `loop_bw` and the default `ted_gain` 1.0 mean the same at every `sps`; the tests run with the block defaults.

I35-1 (`am_mod_fallback` above liquid-dsp's `ampmodem` at `sse2`, `sse4.2` and `neon`) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D586: the gap is accepted, because the extra work of the contract keeps the output bounded and finite for inputs beyond full scale and NaN; the ratio and accuracy waivers of `benches/limits.json` stay as accepted waivers.

I39-1 (the AFC within 50 Hz only on a balanced signal without noise) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D585: test 4 checks the convergence on the idle preamble and the packet error rate with AFC on whitened packets; an AFC that also follows a drift on random data (a second-order loop or an update gated by the preamble) is in the backlog.

I37-2 (packets cannot cross a split of a pipeline into groups, and version 1 of the wire format has no packet message) is closed on 2026-11-05 by the maintainer's decision D587: it goes to the backlog with the network splits (`docs/milestones/BACKLOG.md`, Later), since groups, `split_by_groups` and the wire format are a prepared extension point without a runtime yet.

I30-1 (the speed and accuracy gaps of the M30 entries) is closed on 2026-11-04 by the maintainer's decisions D320, D326 and D327 to D331: the accuracy of `oscillate_fallback` and the cycle gaps of `mul_fallback` C32 at `avx512`, `mul_real_q15_fallback` at `avx2` and `avx512` and `deinterleave_cq15_fallback` at `avx2` are accepted (none needs a waiver: each is within its instruction gate), `mul` C32 fuses on the FMA levels, the gate of `interleave_cq15_fallback` is confirmed, and `add` CQ15 and `add_real` Q15 delegate to the fallback on wasm32.
