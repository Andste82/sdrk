| M13 | M13-8: `convert_out` to `CU8` is above the release limit at `sse2` (1.35x, 5.42 against 4.02 instructions per sample) against VOLK; that entry is `dev`. Every other `convert_out` entry is `release` (`CU8` 1.05x at `sse4.2`, 1.06x at `avx2`, 0.96x on `neon`, 0.69x at `avx512`; `CS8` 0.99x and 1.02x at `sse2` and `sse4.2`, at most 0.84x elsewhere; `CS16` at most 0.91x) | `CU8` reproduces the f64 rounding with ties to even exactly (decision D7): the level paths convert `y - (0.5 - 2^-25)` with `y = x * 128 + 2^-47` and fix the saturated bytes with one byte bound per value (on x86 a signed pack and two minimums, SSE2 without `pminsb` flipping the sign bits for the signed one), which still costs a few instructions per vector more than VOLK's `x * 128 + 127.5` conversion. Decision D7 allows dropping NaN as zero; it is kept, because the output for NaN would then depend on the level of a bit-exact kernel (NEON and SIMD128 clear NaN for free), and `CU8` at `sse2` would still miss the release limit by about 0.1 instructions per sample | Decide whether the exact `U8` rounding and NaN as zero may cost this (then these entries stay `dev` or get a waiver), or whether NaN may give an unspecified byte on x86 (`docs/kernels.md`, the fallback and the golden vectors would document it), or whether a cheaper exact formulation is to be found (the kernel file is `crates/backends/sdrk-backend-simd/src/convert_out.rs`). |
