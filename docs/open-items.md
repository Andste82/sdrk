## I44.1.1-4: survey of the composites (M44.1.1 §5)

Every composite block other than the five QAM and PSK composites of M44.1.1 (`qam_mod`, `psk_mod`, `qam_demod`, `qam_frame_encode`, `qam_frame_decode`), checked against §1 of M44.1.1 (D1818). "Converted" means the composite computes, from a public parameter, a value that a building block takes under another name, in another unit or as a derived value (taps, gains, poles), instead of passing the parameter unchanged to a building block that has it (rules 2 and 4).
