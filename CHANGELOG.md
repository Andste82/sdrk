### Added

- M40.1: `costas` order 8 (8PSK): `CostasState::new` accepts 8 (it was `Unsupported`); a decision-directed detector `im(y conj d)` against the 8PSK point `d` of the half-open octant of `y` and the lock metric of M40.1 §1 (`cos(2 err)`, which reports lock on noise, open item I40.1-1); golden case `costas/8psk_lock_c32_8192` (seed 560); the count entries `costas` and `costas_fallback` recounted.
- M40.1: the `slice` constellation `8psk` (`SliceConstellation::Psk8`, choice index 4; `C32` input, 3 max-log soft bits per symbol, kind cost 16) and `sdrk_blocks::PSK8_GRAY`, the Gray mapping `g -> k` of 8PSK; golden case `blocks/slice_8psk_c32` (seed 0).
- M40.1: `psk_mod` and `psk_demod` take `order` 8 (Gray 8PSK, 3 soft bits per symbol; coherent with `costas` order 8 or differential with the phase in steps of pi/8; ratio `sps / 3`, `psk_demod` kind cost `4 span + 56 / sps`) and the new build parameter `variant` (`sdrk_blocks::PskVariant`: `plain`, the default; `pi4`, pi/4-DQPSK, order 4 and differential only; `offset`, OQPSK, order 4, not differential and an even number of samples per symbol, with the half-symbol delay of the quadrature part in the transmitter and of the in-phase part in the receiver, delays `(span bits + 2) / 2` and `span n / 2 + 1` as for plain QPSK, so a chain counts the half symbol once, D658). Invalid combinations are `InvalidParameter` of `variant` and `invalid_value` in the pipeline. The schema lists the parameter and its options.
- M40.1: the BER and envelope tests of the PSK variants (`sdrk-testkit/tests/psk_ber_m40_1.rs`): 8PSK within 1 dB of its union bound at 12 and 14 dB, pi/4-DQPSK within 1 dB of the DQPSK theory, OQPSK within a factor 1.5 of plain QPSK (carrier on the axes); the envelope minimum of pi/4-DQPSK is 0.135 (open item I40.1-2), that of OQPSK 0.446 of its mean.

### Changed

- M40.1: `PskModConfig` and `PskDemodConfig` have the new public field `variant` (`PskVariant`, default `Plain`): struct literals without `..Default::default()` must name it (D648). The `costas` block, `psk_mod` and `psk_demod` accept `order` 8; order 3 stays `invalid_value`, and orders 5, 6 and 7, which were `out_of_range` above the old maximum of 4, are now `invalid_value` (`InvalidParameter` instead of `OutOfRange`, D640); with order 8 `locked()` reports lock on any input (open item I40.1-1). The OQPSK receiver needs the carrier on the axes (open item I40.1-3).
- M39 (breaking): `sdrk_pipeline::NodeStats` has the new field `readings` and is `PartialEq` without `Eq`.
- M39 (breaking): `sdrk_proto::frame::encode`, `encode_bytes`, `FrameBits::new` and `Decoder::new` take a `Whitening` argument (after `preamble_bytes`, or after `emit_bad` for the decoder); `Whitening::None` gives the frames of M37 unchanged. `FrameEncodeConfig` and `FrameDecodeConfig` have the new field `whitening`.
- M40: `FrameDecodeConfig` has the new public field `polarity` (`FramePolarity`, default `Normal`): struct literals without `..FrameDecodeConfig::default()` must name it (D616). `frame_decode` hunts one extra comparison per bit only with `polarity` both, and inverts the bits of a frame with one XOR per bit.
- `mul` `C32` in the SIMD backend uses FMA on AVX2, AVX-512 and NEON (D326): its contract is an SNR floor of 140 dB against the float32 model instead of bit-exact (149.5 dB on the golden case, 1.6 dB closer to the exact product); the fallback, the reference, SSE2, SSE4.2, SIMD128 and `CQ15` are unchanged and bit-exact. Faster at `neon` (2.27 instead of 3.12 simulated cycles per sample on `cortex-a72`) and `avx512`; at `avx2` 0.45 instead of 0.43 on `znver3`.
- `quad_demod` `C32` in the SIMD backend uses FMA on AVX2, AVX-512 and NEON (D319): faster on every FMA level and 1.2 dB more accurate (144.7 dB against 143.5 dB); the other levels, the fallback and `CQ15` are unchanged.
- M31: `Chain` accepts a pair of streams `Two<X, Y>` (with `X`, `Y` stream elements) between its stages, held in two lockstep buffers; the bound on the intermediate changes from `A::Out: StreamElem` to `A::Out: ChainItem` (sealed; `Frame` and `Packets` are still rejected), and `Chain::storage_bytes` counts both buffers of a pair. `StreamElem` gets the method `slice`, the inverse of `view`.
- M26: the input and the output of `Block` are port types (`Ports`) instead of sample types: a stream of one sample type (`StreamElem`), a pair of streams (`Two<A, B>`) and frames (`Frame`, written through `FrameOut`). `FrameBlock` is removed; `SpectrumBlock` is a `Block<In = T, Out = Frame>`. In `sdrk-pipeline`, `NodeElem` is replaced by `StreamElem` (re-exported from `sdrk-blocks`) and the helper trait `NodeSlice`; `FrameNode<B>` requires `B: Block<Out = Frame>`. No block changes its output. Migration:
  - A stream block does not change: `type In = C32; type Out = C32;` and `fn process(&mut self, input: &[C32], output: &mut [C32]) -> Result<Progress>` still compile. Generic code over `B: Block` that needs slices names the element types, for example `fn run<I: StreamElem, O: StreamElem, B: Block<In = I, Out = O>>(block: &mut B, input: &[I], output: &mut [O])`.
  - A frame block changes from `let (used, meta) = block.push(input, &mut frame)?;` to `let mut meta = None; let used = block.process(input, FrameOut { values: &mut frame, meta: &mut meta })?.consumed;` (`meta` is `Some` when a frame completed; `Progress.produced` is 1 then).

### Fixed

- M40: `Pipeline::pull` with an idle packet source no longer fails with "the packet encoder of a pull made no progress" when a block on the path needs more than the encoder's share of one step before it produces (a QPSK `psk_mod` takes two bits per symbol): the idle encoder is called again, up to 64 times per step (D620).
