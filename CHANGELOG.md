# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the crates follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- M00: workspace with the layer folders, placeholder crates `sdrk-device`, `sdrk-core`, `sdrk` and `sdrk-testkit`, `xtask` (`check`, `bare-metal`, `test-all`), lint and format configuration, `deny.toml`, CI on Linux x86_64, Linux arm64 and Windows x86_64, documentation skeleton, development container (`.devcontainer/`).
- M01: `sdrk-device` with `ErrorKind`/`SdrkError`/`Error`, `SampleFormat` and the `formats` constants, exact time types (`SampleIndex`, `Rate`, `Ratio`, `TimeAnchor`), `BlockFlags`/`BlockMeta` and the `RxStream`/`TxStream` traits; `docs/device.md`.
- M02: `sdrk-core` with the sample types (`C32`, `CQ15`, `F32`, `Q15`), Q15 arithmetic (`num`), `Error`, timing declarations (`Delay`, `Timing`, `Progress`), the one-trait-per-kernel pattern with `Fallback` and `Reference` (feature `reference`), `map_kernel!` and the example kernel `Scale`; facade `sdrk` with `Sdrk`, `delegate!` and `backend_for`; `docs/kernels.md`.
- M03: `sdrk-testkit` foundations: PCG32 (`rng`), signal generators (`r#gen`), metrics, the block-size-invariance harness, the `Arena` bump allocator, the independent raw-format encoder and reference decoder (`encode`), `VirtualRx` with `ToneSource`/`NoiseSource`/`SumSource`, and the suite runner with `WriteReporter`, `StdReporter` (feature `std`) and five registered cases; documentation in `docs/kernels.md`.
- M04: golden-vector infrastructure: `tests/golden` Python generator (`generate.py` with `--all`, `--kernel`, `--check`, `--list`, `--out`), on-disk format version 1, the independent Python Q15 model with self-test, pinned `requirements.txt`, vectors for `q15_ops/*` and `scale/*`, the testkit module `golden` (feature `std`: `load`, `list`, `GoldenCase`, `Tolerance`, `Array`, `assert_matches`, `GoldenError`), the CI job `golden-check`, `tests/golden/README.md` and a section in `docs/kernels.md`.
- M05: the first three kernels end to end: `convert`/`convert_real` (raw device bytes to `C32`/`CQ15`/`f32`/`Q15`, all formats, with the zero-copy views `view_cq15` and `view_c32`), `rotate` with the NCO state `Nco` and build-time cosine tables, and the complex FIR `fir` with real taps and `FirState`; fallback, reference and facade delegation (`delegate!` now accepts generic methods), golden vectors for all three, nine new suite cases, `docs/timing.md` part 1 and the contracts in `docs/kernels.md`.
- M06: the receive kernels: `fir_decimate`, the rational polyphase `resample` and `resample_real`, the FM discriminator `quad_demod`, `deemphasis`, `dc_block`, `window`, `mag_sq` and `power_db` for `C32`/`CQ15` and `f32`/`Q15` with states, fallback, reference and facade delegation; `sdrk_core::window` with `hann`, `blackman_harris`, `kaiser`, `bessel_i0` and `window_gain`; golden vectors for all of them, 21 new suite cases, invariance tests with 20 chunk plans per stateful kernel, and the contracts in `docs/kernels.md` and `docs/timing.md`.
- M07: the FFT as an ordinary kernel: `FftPlan`, `RealFftPlan` and the traits `Fft` (`C32`, `CQ15`; power-of-two sizes 16 to 65 536, in-place radix-2, fixed `1/N` and block-floating-point scaling with the one `FftScale` exponent rule) and `RealFft` (`rfft`/`irfft` for `f32`) with fallback, the naive f64 DFT in `Reference` (`Reference::dft`), facade delegation, the optional feature `static-twiddles` (`TWIDDLES_C32_4096`, `TWIDDLES_CQ15_4096`, `FftPlan::from_static_4096`), 18 golden cases, 12 suite cases, property and extreme-input tests, and the FFT section of `docs/kernels.md`.
- M08: the transmit kernels and the fused kernels: `convert_out`/`convert_out_real` (`C32`, `CQ15`, `f32`, `Q15` to raw device bytes in every format, round half up with saturation), `fir_interpolate`/`fir_interpolate_real` with `FirInterpState` and `FirInterpRealState`, `fm_mod` with `FmModState`, the fused traits `Ddc`, `Duc` and `FmDemod` with the chaining functions `ddc_chained`, `duc_chained` and `fm_demod_chained` (`sdrk_core::fused`) and `FirDecimState::max_input_for_output`, for `Fallback`, `Reference` and the facade (`backend_for` reports the fused kernels as `chained`); the testkit modules `fm` (`fm_baseband`), `meters` (`ToneMeter`, `sinad_db`, `fit_frequency`, `estimate_frequency`, `spectral_mask`), `design_helpers` (`lowpass_hann`) and `e2e` (the streaming FM receive chain and the FM transmitter); 36 golden cases (`convert_out`, `fir_interpolate`, `fir_interpolate_real`, `fm_mod`), 21 suite cases including the end-to-end cases `e2e_fm_rx_*` and `e2e_fm_txrx_*` (f32 and Q15, 0.1 s quick and 1.0 s full), kernel, meter and end-to-end tests, and the contracts in `docs/kernels.md` and `docs/timing.md` (transmit lead time).
- M09: the crate `sdrk-blocks` (layer `crates/blocks`, heap-free): filter design (`firwin_lowpass`, `kaiserord`, `kaiser_beta`, `to_f32`, `to_q15`, `DesignSpec`, checked against SciPy), the storage abstraction (`Storage`, `ArenaStorage`, `AlignedBytes`, `ARENA_ALIGN`; the testkit adds `VecStorage`), the uniform block interface (`Block`, `FrameBlock`, `BlockTiming`, `FrameMeta`, `ParamValue`) with mandatory machine-readable descriptions (`BlockDescription`, `all_descriptions`) and the eleven blocks `convert`, `convert_out`, `scale`, `dc_block`, `fir`, `ddc`, `duc`, `fm_demod`, `fm_mod`, `resampler` (rational and ppm-tunable, complex and real) and `spectrum`, each with a config (`validate`, `set`, `get`), `new`, `storage_bytes` and `rate_out`; the kernels `dot` and `dot_real` (`Fallback`, `Reference`, facade); the testkit function `e2e::measure_rx_blocks`; 27 golden cases (`design`, `dot`, `blocks`), eight suite cases, tests for descriptions, storage, parameters, timing, ppm, chunk invariance and extreme inputs, and `docs/blocks.md`, `docs/timing.md` and `docs/kernels.md`.
- M10: the crate `sdrk-pipeline` (layer `crates/pipeline`, heap allowed): the JSON data model (`PipelineSpec`, `Settings`, `InputSpec`, `NodeSpec`, `EdgeSpec`, `OutputSpec`, format names and custom formats, exact rates as integers or `"num/den"`, canonical output), the block registry (`Registry`, `BlockFactory`, `Checked`, one factory per block kind), the versioned JSON Schema generated from the block descriptions (`schema/sdrk-pipeline-v1.json`, `cargo xtask schema [--check]`), two-stage validation with machine-readable diagnostics (`Report`, `Diagnostic`, `DiagCode`, `Report::to_json`), the build step (automatic `convert` and `convert_out` nodes, topological order, shared buffers with zero-copy fan-out, limits, cost and memory estimate) and the push-mode runner (`Pipeline` with `push`, `read_stream`, `read_raw`, `read_frame`, `available`, `set_param`, `config`, `stats`, `reset`, `report`, stream and frame outputs with the drop policies `error`, `drop_oldest` and `drop_newest`); no allocation after `build()`. Also `VecStorage`, the type-erased `Node` with `StreamNode` and `FrameNode`, three example flows in `examples/flows`, the invalid-flow corpus (one file per diagnostic code), tests against hand-wired blocks, block-size invariance, no-allocation, property tests, a fuzz target and its nightly CI job, and `docs/pipeline.md`.
- M11: the pipeline gets its time base and the transmit path. Sample-index domains per stream with the exact mapping rule (`Block::map_index`, `Node::map_index`; the tuned resampler maps with its position step), `Pipeline::push_with_meta` with gaps (reset and moved origins, equal to two separately reset runs), joining mid-stream, flags and delay-compensated anchors as events (`Event`, `EventKind`, `poll_event`, `NodeId`, `OutputId`, a bounded event queue with `EventsLost`), `input_index`, `read_index` and `time_of`; timed parameters (`set_param_at`, `ParamTime`, exact splitting of the node's `process` call, `ParamApplied` acknowledgements, `late`, `pending_params`); `Latency` per output (`latency`, `Report.outputs`); pull mode (`PullSource`, `pull`, `pull_samples`, `begin_burst`, `end_burst`, `lead_time`, `device_status`, the output setting `underrun`); node groups and backend preferences in the model (`groups`, `nodes.<name>.group`, `nodes.<name>.backend`, the diagnostics `unknown_group` and `backend_unavailable`, `Report.group_edges`, `split_by_groups`); YAML input (feature `yaml`, `serde-saphyr`: `PipelineSpec::from_yaml`, `Registry::validate_yaml`, `Registry::build_yaml`); the versioned wire format (module `wire`, `docs/wire-format.md`, committed example messages); the example flows `fm_receiver_grouped.json` and `fm_transmitter.json` and a YAML twin of every example flow; tests for gaps, anchors, joining, events, timed parameters, no allocation with metadata and pull, pull mode and bursts, groups, YAML, the wire format (with property tests) and the two-thread probe; the sections 'Index domains', 'Latency' and 'Transmit' of `docs/timing.md` and 'Partitioning' of `docs/architecture.md`.
- M12: phase 3 is complete. `sdrk-device` gets the control capabilities (`Range`, `Direction`, `Capabilities`, `Device`, `Tuner`, `RateControl`, `GainControl`, `BandwidthControl`, `HardwareTime`, `TimedCommands`, `Command`, `CommandTime`) and the async streams `AsyncRxStream` and `AsyncTxStream`, with the SoapySDR mapping in `docs/device.md`; the new crate `sdrk-io` (layer `crates/io`) reads and writes raw files (`RawReader`, `RawWriter`), SigMF recordings with captures, gaps, anchors, retunes and annotations (`SigmfReader`, `SigmfWriter`, `Annotation`, `Capture`, `parse_iso8601_ns`, `format_iso8601_ns`) and WAV files (`WavReader`, `WavWriter`); the testkit gets `VirtualTx`, the conformance checks `conformance::check_rx` and `check_tx` (they pass for `VirtualRx`, `VirtualTx` and every reader and writer of `sdrk-io`) and the loopback channel simulator `channel::Loopback` (delay with a fractional-delay filter, attenuation, frequency offset, noise, timed bursts); `Pipeline::input_rate` and `Pipeline::output_rate`; the examples `fm-receiver` and `fm-transmitter` (`examples/README.md`); the workspace member `sdrk-system-tests` (`tests/system`) with the phase 3 system tests (FM over the loopback in f32 and Q15, burst timing, two stations, a long run without drift from index 2^50 with a nightly ten-minute variant, exact event propagation, SigMF round trip through the pipeline); the golden cases `io/*` (SigMF metadata, WAV files, 30 ISO 8601 timestamps), the CI job `system-long`, and `docs/io.md` and `docs/testing.md`.
- M13: phase 4. The crate `sdrk-backend-simd` (layer `crates/backends`, heap-free, no `unsafe`; built on `fearless_simd` 1.1.0) with `convert` and `convert_out` (`U8`, `I8`, little-endian `I16`; bit-exact), `rotate` (`C32` with a polynomial oscillator, `CQ15` bit-exact), `fir`, `fir_decimate`, `fir_interpolate` and `resample` (dot products over prepared taps; `C32` within 110 dB, `CQ15` bit-exact), `mag_sq` (bit-exact), `quad_demod` (vector `atan2`), `fm_mod` (bit-exact) and the in-place FFT (`C32` within 110 dB, `CQ15` bit-exact with the same block-floating-point exponent) for x86-64 SSE2, AVX2 with FMA and AVX-512, AArch64 NEON and wasm32 SIMD128. The level is fixed at compile time or, with the feature `std`, detected at run time. The facade gets the feature `simd` (default), the constant `sdrk::SIMD_BACKEND`, the cfg flag `sdrk_fallback_only` and `backend_for` reports `simd`; `sdrk-core` exposes the state parts and the cosine tables that backends need. The testkit gets ten cases `backend_vs_fallback_*`. `benches/` (a Cargo workspace of its own with `volk-sys`, the kernels and the pipeline benchmark), `benches/limits.json`, `benches/results`, `cargo xtask count --platform <x86_64|x86_64-avx512|aarch64|wasm32>` and `--pipeline` (callgrind, Intel SDE, QEMU with the `insn` plugin, wasmtime fuel; differential counts against VOLK, rustfft and the fallback with an accuracy rule), the CI jobs `avx512-sde`, `aarch64-qemu`, `wasm-wasmtime`, `count` and `count-nightly`, and the documents `docs/backends.md` and `docs/performance.md`. Not met: the `convert_out` and `C32` FFT limits and the release 0.1 (open items M13-1 to M13-4).
- M14: phase 5. The WebAssembly bindings: the crate `sdrk-wasm` (`bindings/wasm`, `cdylib` and `rlib`, `wasm-bindgen` pinned to `=0.2.129`) exposes the pipeline to JavaScript: `version()`, `schema()`, `validate()` (never throws; syntax errors are diagnostics), `backends()` and the class `Pipeline` with `fromJson`, `push` (typed arrays copied once into a buffer allocated at build; metadata with `bigint` indices), `pull` with a JavaScript source, `readStream`, `readFrame`, `available`, `chunkSize`, `dropped`, `setParam` (now, at an input index or at an absolute time), `pollEvent`, `beginBurst`/`endBurst`, `latency`, `config`, `report`, `stats`, `reset` and `free`; failures throw `SdrkError` with `kind`, `code`, `path` and `diagnostics`. The plain-Rust `engine` module behind it is tested on the host. The npm package `sdrk-wasm` (`bindings/wasm/pkg-template`, assembled by `cargo xtask wasm-pkg [--relaxed] [--no-opt] [--no-pack]` without `wasm-pack`, `wasm-opt -O3` when present, about 0.95 MiB per variant) has a loader that picks the SIMD128 or the scalar build at load time (`init()`), an explicit relaxed-SIMD build, the `FlowBuilder` (same model and canonical text as the JSON files), hand-written TypeScript declarations and a README with the Worker pattern. `cargo xtask wasm-test [--env node|chrome|firefox] [--variant ...]` runs the `wasm-bindgen-test` tests. The backend and the facade get the feature `relaxed-simd` (relaxed multiply-add in the `C32` kernels `fir`, `fir_decimate`, `fir_interpolate`, `resample` and `mag_sq`, and through `fearless_simd` in `rotate` and `fft`, on wasm32 with `+relaxed-simd`; not bit-exact). `sdrk-pipeline` gets `Pipeline::input_port` and `Pipeline::output_port`. Tests: engine tests, `wasm-bindgen-test` tests in Node, Chrome and Firefox, JavaScript tests on the packed tarball, and the committed fixture `bindings/wasm/tests/fixtures/fm_receiver.json` that pins the audio of the fallback bit for bit (the scalar WASM build reproduces its SHA-256; the SIMD builds stay within 0.5 dB SINAD). CI: the jobs `wasm-bindings` and `wasm-package`, a relaxed entry in `wasm-wasmtime`. `docs/wasm.md`.
- M15: phase 6 is complete. The testkit suite runs bare-metal in emulators. Five target projects below `targets/` (outside the main workspace, each with its own `.cargo/config.toml`, `Cargo.lock` and the build profile `emu`; shared code in `targets/common`): `qemu-cortex-m4` (tier 1, `qemu-system-arm -machine mps2-an386`), `qemu-cortex-m55` (`mps3-an547`), `qemu-riscv32` (`virt`, scalar fallback, soft float), `esp32p4` (`esp-emu`) and `esp32s3` (Espressif QEMU fork), with semihosting or UART0 reporters, linker scripts and panic handlers that print `SDRK-PANIC`; the cargo feature `full` selects the full profile and `qemu-cortex-m4` has the feature `inject-failure`. `cargo xtask emu [--target <name>|--tier 1|--tier 2|--all] [--full] [--features a,b] [--timeout <s>] [--skip-missing] [--install] [--list]` builds, makes the image (`espflash save-image --merge`), runs the emulator with a timeout, writes `target/emu/<name>.log`, judges the run from the protocol lines only (`xtask::emu::judge`) and prints a summary table; `--install` downloads the pinned `esp-emu` 0.49.0 and Espressif QEMU `esp-develop-9.2.2-20260417` (SHA-256 verified), `espflash` 4.6.0, `espup` 0.18.0 and the Xtensa Rust toolchain 1.95.0.0. `cargo xtask test-all` now also runs the tests of the WebAssembly package (when Node.js is installed) and `emu --tier 1`. `cargo xtask count --platform cortex-m4` counts the instructions per sample of eleven fallback kernels under `qemu-system-arm` with the `insn` plugin and writes `benches/results/cortex-m4.json` (report only). Tests: parser, selection and process handling in `xtask/tests/emu.rs`; the 256 KiB host test of the suite already exists. CI: the jobs `emu-tier1` (with the `inject-failure` self-check), `emu-tier2` (nightly, one job per target, quick and full) and `count-embedded`. `docs/embedded.md`.
- M15: cargo feature `hardware` of `targets/esp32s3` (calls `esp_hal::init` instead of the emulator set-up), `cargo xtask emu --build-only` and the CI job `esp32s3-hardware-build` that compiles both configurations.

### Changed

- M13: the minimum Rust version of the workspace is 1.89 (the MSRV of `fearless_simd`). With the default feature `simd` of the facade, the `C32` kernels `rotate`, `fir`, `fir_decimate`, `fir_interpolate`, `resample`, `quad_demod` and `fft` give results that meet their SNR contract but are no longer bit-identical with `Fallback` (the bit-exact kernels and all `CQ15` kernels but `quad_demod` still are); `RUSTFLAGS="--cfg sdrk_fallback_only"` restores the fallback everywhere. `sdrk-pipeline` turns on run-time level detection (`sdrk/std`). A backend preference `{"require": "simd"}` now succeeds for nodes whose kernels are accelerated.
- M14: `C32` `mag_sq` is no longer bit-exact with the fallback in the relaxed WebAssembly build (feature `relaxed-simd` with `+relaxed-simd`); in every other build nothing changes. The tests of the backend and the facade accept a few ulp there.
- M15: the root `rust-toolchain.toml` lists the targets `thumbv8m.main-none-eabihf`, `riscv32imac-unknown-none-elf` and `riscv32imafc-unknown-none-elf`; the root `Cargo.toml` excludes `targets/*` from the workspace; `xtask` depends on `sha2` (checksums of the downloaded emulators).
